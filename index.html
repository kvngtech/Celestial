Questions

Is this real astrology?

It is traditional sun-sign astrology, shared for entertainment. There is no scientific evidence that star signs predict personality or events.

Do I need to enter my birth year?

No. Your sun sign depends only on the month and day you were born.

Another site shows a different sign for my birthday. Why?

The sun moves into a new sign on a slightly different day and time each year, usually between the 20th and 23rd. If you were born on or next to a change date, a birth chart with your time and place will settle it. We use the standard dates.

Do you save my birthday?

Not unless you tick "Remember my birthday on this device". Then it stays in your own browser, and you can remove it with "Forget my birthday". It is never sent to us or anyone else. The calendar reminder file is also built on your device.

How is the daily reading written?

It is a short, general note chosen from a written set by the date. Everyone with your sign sees the same one that day. It is for fun, not advice.

How is compatibility worked out?

From the traditional relationship between two signs on the wheel, plus their elements and modalities. It is a way to explore, not a verdict on any relationship.
